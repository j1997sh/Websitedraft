REPORT LANDING PAGE CONCEPT

About this publication

Easing the Burdens is a Centre for Policy Studies publication. This demonstration shows how its online presentation could be developed beyond a simple PDF download, without altering or inventing the report's findings.

Read the evidence in full

For the report’s actual findings, data, methodology and recommendations, open the original CPS publication using the button below.

Open full report (PDF) ↗

A better digital reading experience

A live report page could provide an executive summary, clearly sourced key findings, author commentary, accessible charts and links to relevant interviews. Each element would be reviewed against the published report before going live.

Make the research easier to discover

Readers could move between the report, its source material, related commentary and video explainers. This gives research a longer life beyond publication day and creates opportunities for distribution through email, social media and the Media Hub.

FROM REPORT TO VIDEO

Watch, listen and explore

Connect publications to author interviews, short-form explainers, podcast discussions and event recordings. Media examples in the current prototype are illustrative.

Digital campaign opportunities

  • Author-led 60-second explanation of the central policy question.
  • Research graphics and charts checked against the original source.
  • Behind the Research interview with the report author.
  • Newsletter edition directing readers to the full publication.
  • Relevant CapX commentary where editorially appropriate.
Source integrity: This concept intentionally does not reproduce unverified statistics, quotes or recommendations. The official PDF is the source of record. Photography is illustrative.
